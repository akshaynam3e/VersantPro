❤️ A Small Message From the Developer

Thank you for being part of the VersantPro community. What started as a small personal project has now grown into a platform used by hundreds of learners. Seeing students improve their English communication skills and prepare for placements motivates me to continue building and improving VersantPro every day. VersantPro is currently built, maintained, and updated entirely by a single student developer. As some of you may know, I am currently under 18 years of age. Because of this, I am not yet able to officially integrate supporter subscriptions and donation systems in my own name. After September 2026, once I turn 18, I plan to introduce an optional Supporter Program for users who would like to help contribute toward hosting costs, Firebase services, maintenance, infrastructure, question bank updates, and future development. All current practice sections will continue to remain completely free. The Supporter Program will simply be a voluntary way for community members to support the platform if they find it useful. Supporters will receive special profile badges, supporter levels, contribution history, community recognition, and early access to future features. There is absolutely no obligation to contribute. Your feedback, suggestions, and continued use of VersantPro are already a huge source of support and motivation. Thank you for helping this project grow and for being part of the journey. 🚀❤️
