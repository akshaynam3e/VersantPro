How the Real Versant Test Works & How to Score 70+

Complete guide for all 6 sections. Passing score starts from 47/80.

🏆 Score Bands

Score Level
70 – 80 Expert
60 – 69 Advanced
47 – 59 ✅ Passing
35 – 46 Below Pass
0 – 34 Beginner

📚 Read

  • Read naturally without rushing.
  • Pronounce every word clearly.
  • Pay attention to punctuation.
  • Avoid unnecessary pauses.
  • Practice reading English articles aloud daily.

🎤 Repeat Sentence

  • Listen carefully to every word.
  • Repeat immediately after hearing the sentence.
  • Do not add or skip words.
  • Maintain a stable speaking pace.
  • Focus on pronunciation and accuracy.

💬 Conversation

  • Listen for important details.
  • Answer only what is asked.
  • Do not add extra information.
  • Use complete and natural sentences.
  • Focus on clarity more than speed.

⌨️ Dictation

  • Use correct capitalization.
  • Pay attention to punctuation.
  • Listen for contractions such as don't and it's.
  • Type every word accurately.
  • Review before submitting.

✏️ Fill In The Blanks

  • Read the entire sentence first.
  • Use grammar and context clues.
  • Check tense and sentence structure.
  • Choose the word that fits naturally.
  • Avoid guessing too quickly.

📖 Passage Reconstruction

  • Remember facts instead of exact wording.
  • Focus on who, what, when, where and why.
  • Include all key information.
  • Never invent new details.
  • Use your own words while keeping the original meaning.

📞 Call Handling Tips

  • Begin with open-ended questions.
  • Use closed-ended questions to confirm details.
