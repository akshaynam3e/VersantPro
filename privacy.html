Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information VersantPro may process, why it is used, and the choices available to users of the English fluency practice platform.

Last updated: September 2026

Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, VersantPro may process an email address and display name for account access, practice responses or progress information needed to provide the service, and technical information such as browser or device details when needed to diagnose errors.

How information is used

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To save practice progress or preferences when those features are enabled.
  • To respond to support requests and investigate technical problems.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, and improve the reliability of the platform.

We do not claim that practice data can be used to predict an official assessment score.

Service providers

VersantPro uses third-party infrastructure such as Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy documentation. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described in the applicable Google policies and consent controls.

Cookies and local storage

The website may use essential browser storage to keep a session, remember preferences, or support basic functionality. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings, although disabling essential storage may prevent login or saved progress from working correctly.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask questions about your information, request correction of inaccurate details, or ask about deletion where applicable. Requests should be sent from the account email address when possible. Some records may need to be retained when required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact versantpro.in@gmail.com. This general policy is not legal advice and may be updated when the service or applicable requirements change.
